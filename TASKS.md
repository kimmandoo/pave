## Reference-derived implementation backlog

Source paths below are relative to the inspected sibling reference tree. Tasks describe useful behavior to build in native OCaml, not instructions to copy its Bun, npm, Bazel, Rust/N-API, hosted service or branding. Each item remains open until its user-facing scenario and security boundary are verified. The earlier checked items are Pave's **current baseline**, not full equivalence.
